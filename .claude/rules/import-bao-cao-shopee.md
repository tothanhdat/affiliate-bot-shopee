**Ten cot do Shopee viet hoa "G" va "H" giua cau trong khi 2 cot ngay ke ben viet thuong ("Thời gian hoàn thành", "Thời gian Click") - da doi chieu file that, DUNG "sua lai cho dong nhat"**. **Quet 14 bao cao that (2026-09-10) chi thay dung 4 gia tri: "Hoan thanh" (44 dong), "Dang cho xu ly" (23), "Chua thanh toan" (1), "Da huy" (1)** - rieng "Khong hop le" chua tung xuat hien lan nao trong du lieu that (giu lai vi da co san). **Nhanh "Da huy" co BANG QUYET DINH 5 ca tu 2026-10-08** (truoc do CHI ca `pending` duoc xu li, 4 ca con lai roi het vao 1 dong warning - nen entry `confirmed` CHUA rut cung khong bi reverse, tuc tien LAY LAI DUOC ma van nam trong Kha dung cua user va rut duoc binh thuong; day la lo hong lon hon ca chuyen "user da rut"): `pending` -> reverse nhu cu; `confirmed` + `withdrawal_id IS NULL` (**ke ca don dang bi giam**) -> reverse, **thu hoi TRON, khong no**; `confirmed` + dang nam trong yeu cau rut `requested` -> **TU DONG huy ca yeu cau rut** (`cancelWithdrawal`) roi reverse don, thu hoi TRON, khong no, DM user ly do (2026-10-08, yeu cau truc tiep cua user - DAO NGUOC quyet dinh ban dau "ghi no ngay + canh bao admin"; rui ro da chap nhan: admin DA chuyen khoan tay ma chua bam "da tra" thi tien bi tinh nhu chua chuyen, xem doc comment `cancelledWithdrawals`); `paid` -> ghi no + DM user; `reversed` -> bo qua im lang.
